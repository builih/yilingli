We investigate camera-in-the-loop (CITL) computer-generated holography (CGH) for 3D nanoscale printing in photosensitive resin by training neural networks to compensate for optical aberrations in a 4f metasurface–spatial light modulator optical system. We further correct for speckle, pixel cross-talk, and zeroth-order light to improve agreement between simulated and experimental holograms.
