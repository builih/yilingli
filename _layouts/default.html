Yiling Li

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Purdue University and a member of the Intelligent Imaging Lab (i2Lab) advised by Prof. Stanley Chan.

My research focuses on advancing light-driven technologies at the intersection of nanophotonics and computational imaging. I am particularly interested in how nanoscale structures, material systems, and algorithmic models can be co-designed to control light through metasurface engineering and holographic display architectures for next-generation optoelectronic devices and display systems.

I received my B.S.E. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Princeton University with minors in Statistics & Machine Learning, Robotics, and Visual Arts. My undergraduate senior thesis on differentiable forward models for holographic display systems was advised by Prof. Felix Heide at Princeton Computational Imaging Lab.

Beyond research, I enjoy tinkering with electronics, creating art, and learning dance choreography.
